How the patterns are counted

Each model has five possible actions for each cue. Encounters in this study specify a preferred action. During distillation, the next model sees selected cues and fits its own action probabilities to the previous model’s outputs. The source keeps its responses. The particles visualize those output probabilities.

Each count is one distinct cue–response pair. In this simulation a pair enters the count when its action reaches 60% probability on that cue. Its origin records how that generation acquired it. For example, “visitor lingers → stay beside” originates in I and appears as inherited in II and III. “Visitor returns → approach” originates in II and appears as inherited in III.

The encounters, cue selection, and threshold are design choices. The displayed values come from this small simulation. The Raspberry Pi is the proposed onboard computer.
