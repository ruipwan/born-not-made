Sources · Valentine et al., Hybrid 3D printing of soft electronics, Adv. Mater. 2017 (parts picked and placed by a vacuum nozzle onto the printed matrix; with a printed droplet under each part, strain to failure rose from 0.14 to 1.22; placement error 136 ± 62 µm, about 10 % of the part size) · Wu, DeConinck, Lewis, Omnidirectional printing of 3D microvascular networks, Adv. Mater. 2011 (a filler fluid fills the voids the nozzle leaves in the reservoir) · Bhattacharjee et al., Writing in the granular gel medium, Sci. Adv. 2015 (the gel yields at the tip and turns solid again behind it; 0.2 % Carbopol, yield stress 9 Pa) · Hinton et al., 3D printing PDMS elastomer in a hydrophilic support bath via freeform reversible embedding, ACS Biomater. Sci. Eng. 2016 (silicone and Carbopol do not mix; the print cures in the bath for up to 72 hours) · Wehner et al., An integrated design and fabrication strategy for entirely soft, autonomous robots, Nature 2016 (a premade controller set on pins in the mold, its inlets plugged, the matrix cast and cured around the controller) · Espalin et al., 3D printing multifunctionality: structures with electronics, Int. J. Adv. Manuf. Technol. 2014 (the FDM print stops, wires are embedded, the print goes on) · Lai et al., On-ratio PDMS bonding for multilayer microfluidic device fabrication, J. Micromech. Microeng. 2019 (partly cured layers bond for good when cured together) · Smooth-On, Dragon Skin series technical bulletin (Dragon Skin 30: pot life 45 minutes, cure 16 hours; tin cure silicone, latex and sulfur stop the cure).

Open questions · No source places a part inside a gel bath during a silicone print; this is untested. Fresh Dragon Skin 30 on a fully cured capsule may not bond, so the flange holes give a lock that does not need a bond. A gel film may stay on the pod after the pass through the gel. The potting inside the pod must hold no tin or sulfur. The holder must let go without lifting the pod out of the wet bed. How long electronics stay dry in silicone under water is not known. The capped ports of a pump pod are untested.
