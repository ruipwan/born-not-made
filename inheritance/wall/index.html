One part, into the wall

No electronic part sits in the gel or on the gel floor. Every part except the arm ends up sealed between the two skins of the double wall. This page follows the one pod of the newborn from the dry magazine in the tail into one patch of the wall, at full scale. The pod is the whole kit on one aluminum plate: a Raspberry Pi 5, its sensors, a motor driver, two water pumps, a cell and a regulator, all wired on the plate and cast in silicone. No wire runs in the print. Only water crosses the skin, through four capped tubes. The wall is cut open through the middle of the pod, so the inside is visible. The view is a time lapse; the clock shows process time.

Carbopol is a solid below its yield stress, so an empty cup in the print stays full of gel. The bed of fresh silicone pushes the gel out first. The pocket is filled solid; it is not an air cell. Fresh silicone may not bond to the cured capsule, so it locks through the holes in the flange. The tapered rim centers the pod after the tool change. Each pump needs a capped tube for its inlet and one for its outlet.
