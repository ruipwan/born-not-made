1 · Chemical

Baking soda and acid

What it needs to make it

  • A tablet of 29.7 g: sodium bicarbonate 16.8 g and citric acid 12.8 g, in a dry pocket of 42 × 42 × 16 mm.
  • 89 mL of tank water, metered at 1.5 mL/min by the AITIAO 3 to 5 V micro submersible pump through an orifice, so about 59 min.
  • Makes 8.8 g of CO₂, which is 4.76 L. The 17.2 g of sodium citrate leaves in solution with the jet.
  • 3 NaHCO₃ + C₆H₈O₇ → Na₃C₆H₅O₇ + 3 H₂O + 3 CO₂

Risk. CO₂ dissolves in water and passes through silicone much faster than air, so the body slowly shrinks unless it is fed again with a new tablet.

2 · Mechanical

Mini air pump

What it needs to make it

  • Adafruit ZR370-02PM air pump: 4.5 V, 2.5 L/min free flow, about 500 mA, rated for 10 s on and 5 s off.
  • At about 1.6 kPa back pressure, take about 2.3 L/min: 124 s of pumping, 3.1 min on the clock.
  • About 17 mAh of the 500 mAh LiPo for the pump, through a boost converter from 3.7 to 4.5 V.
  • A snorkel to a float above the water, with a check valve so water and gas cannot run back.

Risk. The inlet must stay above water. If the float tips under, the pump draws water, and the motor is the part most likely to wear out.

3 · Electrical

Water electrolysis

What it needs to make it

  • A cell with two electrodes in water with sodium bicarbonate as electrolyte. Not salt: salt water makes chlorine.
  • Faraday’s law: 0.200 mol of gas needs 0.267 mol of electrons, 25.8 kC, which is 7.16 Ah. It splits 2.4 g of water into 3.17 L H₂ and 1.59 L O₂.
  • At 1 A that is 7.2 h. The 500 mAh kit LiPo alone makes 0.33 L, 7% of the target, in 30 min, then it is empty.
  • About 8 Ah more: for example three 18650 cells, or power from outside the body.

Risk. Hydrogen and oxygen mixed 2 to 1 is a flammable, explosive mixture. Inside the body it must be kept away from any spark, and the body holds 4.76 L of it.
