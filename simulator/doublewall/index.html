Every head a quilted double membrane

One head form for every generation: the head of the simple version (the spindle head, the roof falling in one arc to the mouth, the oval mouth leaning back, the long lower lip, the gel held by its own lower part, the docking face; in side view the lower outline is one smooth line: the floor of the lobe flat on the tank floor, a fillet, the front wall, a second fillet and the lip rising 3 degrees to the rolled mouth edge), made as an inflated double membrane of silicone, quilted like a down jacket: two skins joined by short webs into small irregular cells, smallest at the docking rim and the mouth, largest on the roof, one depth along the lower line, their direction turning along the body. The interior is open. The first head is made that way beforehand, outside the tank; every newborn is such a head. Only the tail (arm, tools, store) stays a hard print.

The lower part of the head was enlarged so that the next head fits there folded flat: the gel lobe is 224 mm wide instead of 96, the rest of the form is unchanged. The arm prints the next head folded flat, 18 layers, around its kit, in the gel of the head. The birth (round 2) is keel first and mouth end first: a band of two tubes along the floor centerline of the newborn is filled first; it straightens the folds and carries the push. The soft strip passes the mouth cupped, its edges riding up the lower contour of the mouth. The holder pushes the rim while air is fed into the cells from the mouth end, so each section opens across only outside the lip; a folding crank on the holder carries the rim the last part, 20 mm past the lip; the rim fills last. The newborn slides off the lip and lies in front of the mouth, a quilted head of the same form. The relay and the next cycle are not run in this round.

Point by point

How each dimension is set

    How this is simulated

    The Python pipeline of the simple version (bnm_design/quilt/sim/design.py, round 11) builds the tail, plans every move of the arm and writes quilt.json; here it runs with the enlarged lower shell and with the next head as its print. A second script (bnm_design/quilt/derive.py) solves the quilt: the cells are placed on the true head surface by dart throwing with a size field and a turning direction field, each cell gets webs and two bulging skins, and the section above the gel is checked as a two hinged arch on the lobe (loads times 2, the ballooning limit of the largest bulge divided by 1.5, wrinkling of the skin on the compression side). The same script tries the birth through the mouth against the true mouth rim and the reach of the DexArm (official joint ranges). The browser replays and interpolates.

    Colliders: the inner skin of the head (the inner face of the simple head, the cells bulge out from it), the saddles, the kit magazine and its spare kits, the parts of the store, the seat, the eight arm links, the module head and stem, the magnet, the parked modules, the kit, the folded next head as it is printed, and the newborn in every frame of the birth (its outer and inner skin against the inner skin and the quilt of the head, the lip, the rolled mouth edge, the tank floor, the arm, the holder and its crank). Every frame is tested for every pair; the Checks panel lists the deepest overlap of each pair.

    What is printed where

    The H2S prints only the tail, in two prints, unchanged from the simple version, and the molds of the first head. The heads are silicone: the first one cast in a two part mold (or printed in a gel bath outside the tank beforehand), every later one printed folded flat by the arm in the gel of its parent.

    The tank

    Staging

    One long glass tank on a dark plinth in a black room, one lamp straight above. The water is nearly still. The switch above the view adds a few slow inner lights; the default is off. None of this changes the physics.
