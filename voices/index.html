An open experiment about a new kind of artificial species. Say what you think of it.

Born, Not Made has a trunk: a mother robot that prints a baby inside her body and gives birth in water. But the trunk is a question, not an answer. This is an ongoing experiment about a new kind of artificial species, and I do not know yet what that species should become.

Branches grow from the trunk, and many of them come from other people. So the discussion is not around the work. It is the work. Leave a few words or a drawing, and they will stay here, in public, next to everyone else's.

Ruipeng Wang

Each small body is a voice, and a drawing floats inside its body. Open one to read it, or touch the water.

Ink

Voices are kept on GitHub, in public, so the record lasts and anyone can see where each one came from; for now you need a free GitHub account to leave one.

In the open

Each voice becomes a public issue in this project's GitHub repository. Anyone can read it, react to it and reply.

Lasting

A small script copies every voice into one data file. Git keeps each version of that file, so the record has a history.

Traceable

Every voice shows who left it and links back to where it came from. The code of this page is open too.

Kept kind

A voice that attacks a person may be hidden from this page. Disagreement is welcome.
