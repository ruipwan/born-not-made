Bodies

The same society as the population page, drawn with the real bodies from the Build page: the hard shell hull, the tail and the baby, floating at the design waterline. Mothers swim slowly, dock by electropermanent magnets, charge, print babies, and raise the babies in turns with other adults. A baby is printed folded, 54 mm long, and the arm brings the baby out of the opening while inflating the baby to 385 mm. The dead stop, lose color and drift into a cluster of clear shells that grows slowly. Click any body to follow.
