Rules

Relation is shared memory, likeness of body and time spent near each other. A robot ready to bear looks for the one it relates to most; at least one of the two has a tail. They touch opening to opening and exchange memory and form. The one with a tail carries: the arm prints the newborn in gel, layer by layer, then inflates it a little inside the chamber and pushes it out. Each newborn mixes both bodies, with drift. If both have tails the newborn mostly has one; with one tail, half the time. Nobody dies; the water feeds only so many, so births slow as the tank fills.
