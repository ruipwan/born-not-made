# A reader in the projection beam

At film time 49.5 s, a visitor standing at (1.40, 4.18) m clears every sampled pixel of the lower caption from the seated eye. With the long throw, the same person intercepts the projector rays to 1,786 of its 2,718 bright pixels. Someone in the bay could see the wall perfectly well and still lose the words. Shifting the lens forward clears the caption in this arrangement. When the person steps another 0.72 m toward the wall both projectors lose much of it.

The shadow comes from intersections between lens-to-screen rays and the head, torso and legs. All 192 frames use actual gallery pictures from film 46–54 s. The comparison looks squarely at the wall. The viewer's separate line of sight is computed in the table. The model uses a point lens, opaque ellipsoids and a neutral ambient floor of 2% of nominal white in linear light. It gives a hard shadow edge and models neither lens scatter nor room photometry. The standard-throw shadow touched the image in 149 of 192 frames, up to 10.3% of the picture area. ## Optical fit and venue decisions

The trial image is 1.90 m wide, about 85.8 inches diagonally at 16:9. Its centre is at 1.485 m and its top at about 2.02 m. Both optical centres sit at x = 1.40 m and z = 2.02 m, with no horizontal shift. The shorter lens is 1.70 m from the image and the longer one is 3.85 m away. A level mount would need vertical shift. The [beam section](beam-section.svg) shows an optical centre only. The projector, mount and cables occupy more space.

Epson's 16:9 distance equations give approximately 2.57–4.18 m for the EB-L630U and 1.52–2.06 m for the EB-L630SU at that picture width. These reference models have 1920 × 1200 panels. Their published vertical-shift ranges cover an optical centre at the active picture's top edge. The standard lens has optical zoom. The short model uses digital zoom above its wide setting. Try its wide position near 1.52 m if keeping the active picture raster is critical. Confirm actual image, lens shift, focus and blank margins with the full-size caption fragments. No equipment has been selected or tested in a venue. [Epson specifications, projection distance formulas and 16:9 tables, pp. 25–29](https://download3.ebz.epson.net/dsc/f/03/00/13/67/06/b6b487e2195fe3e46266495744b017cb6090e335/Specification_EBL735Useries_v104_EN.pdf)

Give the AV installer the tape marks, actual player and two print sheets. Confirm mount position and usable headroom from the installed chassis and circulation route, plus power, cooling, fan noise and operator access. Try the live tank lights, a bright population frame, a visitor crossing and the sharp caption pattern before signing off the wall. A direct-view display on the same wall avoids the front-projector shadow but still has seated occlusion and glass glare to test. No assumed optical centre constitutes a mounting specification.
