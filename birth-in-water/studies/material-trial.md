# Weight in water

In the first shot the pan is full of motion. Ripples pass a shell wrapped in white tape. In the second, the camera briefly loses the floating body. When the camera finds it again a wire leads back to a hand. A person and the bench apparatus remain inside the frame of the experiment. It would take another shot, with independent controls and a fixed camera, to decide how much propulsion each component supplied.

The [26-second film](../media/weight-in-water.mp4) joins Ruipeng Wang's two complete 2025 workshop recordings in the order of his journal. The pink shell occupied the pan floor in his account. A later test put the pump in sponge. The wire, hand and loose electronics in the second recording remain visible. The two demonstrations differ in vessel, support, framing and operator action. The films give the gallery a way to discuss the conditions for movement when the overhead release alone makes the machine appear self-contained.
