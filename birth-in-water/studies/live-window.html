Operator and host handoff

At the fault, the operator stops the demonstration and changes the tank status card. The floor host keeps the service route and seated bay open and points out the recorded film. During a crowded reset, one person cannot both work at the rear bench and reliably attend to visitors in front. If relief is unavailable, keep the tank marked paused until the operator can resume supervision.
