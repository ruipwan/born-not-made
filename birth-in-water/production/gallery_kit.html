Rehearse the room

The film opens inside Water Beings, returns to the workbench and stays with the carrier after the smaller body slips free. An operator prepares the physical apparatus on its own clock. Display its current state beside the tank. These sheets test one possible room. Witness, A Stranger and The Night Shift remain alternative directions.

  1. Print the plan, elevations and observation sheet on two A3 pages. Tape the lateral bay, the standing marks and the service route. Time an actual reset.
  2. Print the A3 caption proof at 100%. Check its 100 mm rule and try the words from the seated bay and entry.
  3. Print the two A4 tank status cards. Place the appropriate face at the tank before opening a supervised window or starting a pause.
